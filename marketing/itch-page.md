## Page description (paste into the body)

**One woman. One kayak. One vape. An entire canal system that wants her gone.**

Angela Rayner's: Vape Kayak starts as a tight, one-thumb endless runner. Tap left, tap right, dodge the barges, hoover up every spinning vape on the water.

Then it stops being a runner.

Every vape is experience. Every level is a choice. A polite little puff of vapour becomes six directions of fog, a toxic vapetrail, hoops the size of a narrowboat, and finally weather. By the time you reach the jungle you are no longer avoiding the swans. The swans are avoiding you.

**What's in the kayak**

- **One-thumb controls.** Tap left or right. That's it. Attacks fire themselves.
- **From vulnerable to unstoppable.** Four cloud weapons, six passives and four evolutions, including the Pea Souper and Hurricane Ange.
- **Three canals.** British towpaths, a desert of sandbars and heat-haze mirages, and a jungle that is mostly teeth.
- **A cast of nuisances.** Swans, paparazzi in dinghies, tumbleweeds, piranhas, mosquitoes, crocodiles, low bridges, lock gates and somebody's second home.
- **A status bar with a face.** She winces, grins, takes a drag and gets steadily more dishevelled as the hearts run out.
- **A shop between runs.** Bank your cash, buy the Vape Engine, go again faster.
- **Crunchy 1-bit pixel art.** Black, white, ginger, and one pale blue vape.
- **Entirely fair and balanced tips** on stamp duty, expenses and which home is your main home.

**Controls**

- Phone: tap or hold the left or right half of the screen. Optional tilt steering in Settings.
- Desktop: arrow keys or A / D to steer, 1 / 2 / 3 to pick an upgrade, P to pause.

**The small print**

This is a free, non-commercial work of parody and satire. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any politician, party, company or vape manufacturer, and nothing in it is a statement of fact. It is not sold, has no ads, takes no donations and makes no money for anyone.

No accounts, no tracking, no data collected: your progress is saved only in your own browser.

All names, likenesses and music belong to their respective owners. Rights holders: message me here and it comes down. No kayaks were harmed.

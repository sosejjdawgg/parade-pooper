FAQ

Can I see your work first?

Yes. All six portfolio games are free to play at solidsosej.itch.io.

Will it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Games are built mobile-first and tested on both, in Safari and Chrome.

Do I own the game?

You get the source code and full rights to the custom work made for you. Third-party music or assets keep their own licences, which I'll always list.

Can you put it on the App Store?

I build web games that play from a link. App-store packaging can be quoted separately.

Can you make a game about a real person or brand?

Personal gifts, your own brand and clearly-labelled non-commercial parody are fine. I won't make content that harasses anyone or misleads people about who made it.

What if my idea is bigger than Premium?

Message me first and I'll send a custom offer with milestones.

# Fiverr portfolio project: Angela Rayner's: Vape Kayak

## Project title
Vape Kayak: Survivor-Like Mobile Runner (Parody Game)

## Description
A one-thumb endless runner that turns into bullet-heaven chaos, designed and built from scratch as a free, non-commercial political parody.

**The loop:** tap left or right to steer a kayak down procedurally generated canals. Collect vapes to level up, pick a new power at every level, and turn a small puff of vapour into screen-filling clouds that melt the horde. It's built for short, replayable runs on a phone.

**The systems:** four weapons with evolutions, ten passive powers, a boost ram attack, a shield, healing tea and a permanent upgrade shop. There are three biomes (canal, desert, volcano), and the banks turn red and deadly later on. Every item and death message carries a satirical joke.

**What I delivered**
- Game design, balancing and progression tuning
- 1-bit pixel art with a reactive HUD portrait
- Mobile-first HTML5 code with touch and tilt controls, running at 60fps with hundreds of effects on screen
- An original chiptune menu theme, seamless music loops and sound effects
- An itch.io launch: store page, key art, social images and a vertical Shorts trailer
- Extra builds: a single-file HTML version, a PICO-8 cart and a port for retro handheld consoles

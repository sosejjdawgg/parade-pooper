# Fiverr portfolio project: BONES

## Project title
BONES: Virtual Pet + Wave Survival Mobile Game

## Description
A two-in-one mobile game I designed, illustrated and built from scratch: raise a rottweiler pup, then fight for him.

**The pet side:** feed, walk and play with BONES, check in on him through the DOGCAM and read his mood on his face. When he grows old, his pup carries on stronger and a memorial wall remembers him.

**The action side:** in the DOGPARK, fight off waves of birds, alpha cats, nut-throwing squirrels and apes. Recruit and upgrade friends, earn moves like leap, whirlwind and judgment, and take on a full boss fight. Beating the park unlocks a night mode, DOGPARK UNLEASHED.

**Bonus game:** a sheep-herding campaign across seven fields, dodging holes, wolves, traffic and eagles all the way up a mountain.

**What I delivered**
- Game design, balancing and progression
- Pixel art, animation and UI, with 11 switchable interface themes
- One-thumb touch controls (paw stick + action tiles), plus mouse and keyboard
- Browser build for phone and desktop, plus an Android app
- Store page, key art and screenshots

Two games in one: a virtual pet you raise and care for, and an arcade wave-survival brawler with a boss fight, plus a bonus sheep-herding campaign. I handled design, pixel art, UI (11 themes), one-thumb touch controls and builds for browser and Android.
